# Code of conduct

Huldra wants to be a friendly place to learn how operating systems work.
People of every background and level of experience are welcome here,
including people asking their very first question about paging or
system calls.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Critique code and ideas, not people. Explain the *why* behind a review
  comment.
- Accept that people know different things. "Read the manual" is not an
  answer; a link to the right part of it is.
- Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks; trolling; deliberately derailing
  discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without their permission.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions,
and anywhere someone represents the project.

Report problems to the maintainers privately, through a
[private report](https://github.com/villetacore/huldra/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting [@villetacore](https://github.com/villetacore). Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other
contributions that break these rules, and may temporarily or permanently
ban anyone who does so repeatedly or seriously.

This code of conduct is based on the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
